Clean up

Blurry shots, pocket shots, screenshots, exact copies and spare burst frames, for you to review and move to the app's trash. The trash keeps the files until you empty it, and nothing is ever deleted from iCloud: a trashed photo that iCloud sends again goes straight back to the trash.

Family

Everyone who pairs a phone (Settings → Phone access) can add their own photos to this library from the phone. Copies already in the library are refused, and each photo is marked with who added it.

Review queue

The app's least-certain guesses (a photo joined to a trip by date only, a face that only loosely matches, a chapter that might not be a move). A minute a week keeps the story reviewed rather than assumed.

Second copy

3-2-1: iCloud, this laptop, and one more drive.

Copy the whole library to another drive and verify every file by checksum. Only new or changed files are copied each time.

Archive

For a USB stick, a family archive or your own web host.

Your whole story as a self-contained web site: chapters with their stories and milestones, trips, events, albums and the best photos of each, as downscaled copies with all metadata removed. Private photos are never included.

Best photos

Runs on this computer, free.

Every photo gets a local quality rating (sharpness, exposure, contrast) and bursts of near-identical shots are grouped so only the best one shows up in replays, books and the year review. Rating happens after each sync; use this to rate older photos now.

Import from a folder

Add photos you have elsewhere (your desktop, an old phone, a camera card). The app checks each file against the library first: exact copies are skipped, and look-alikes (the same picture resized or re-saved) are listed for you to decide. Originals are copied, never moved.
